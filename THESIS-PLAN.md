## Reference — integration time and figures of merit

"Integration time" means two different things:
1. **Lock-in time constant τ** (a knob). Integration time of a single reading; sets
   the detection bandwidth Δf. Single-pole: Δf = 1/(4τ); two-pole: Δf = 1/(8τ).
2. **Allan averaging time** (an analysis variable). Record one long stream, compute
   the deviation vs averaging window; the minimum gives the optimal integration time.

NNEA = α_min · P / √Δf  [cm⁻¹ · W · Hz^(−1/2)], where α_min is the SNR = 1
absorption (from calibration + no-gas noise), P is the optical power at the fork.
Converting concentration to α needs the HITRAN line strength.

Recipe: calibration sweeps at τ ≈ 100 ms–1 s (note τ + slope); Allan at short τ,
log every 0.1–1 s for ≥ 1000 s. Avoid the lock-in ranges that read ~40% high at
τ ≥ 30 ms (§4.2).

## Reference — key numbers

- QTF resonance / modulation: f₀ = 12458.8 Hz
- QCL: Daylight Solutions MIRcat-2300, 1111–2000 cm⁻¹ (5–9 µm)

\section{Theoretical Background}
\label{sec:theory}

Numbered equations are referenced with \cref{eq:fourier}. The continuous-time
Fourier transform is
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:fourier}
  X(f) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x(t)\, e^{-j 2\pi f t}\, \mathrm{d}t .
\end{equation}

Inline math and units work together, e.g. a supply voltage of \SI{3.3}{\volt}
and a sampling rate of \SI{1}{\mega\hertz}.

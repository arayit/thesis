\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Summarize what was accomplished: the design and construction of a pulsed-QCL
QEPAS sensor, the characterization of its tuning fork / ADM, lock-in chain, and
QCL, the optical power budget, and the validated detection of \nto\ with its
measured sensitivity and stability. Restate the headline performance numbers and
why they matter.

\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:future}

Outline the next steps. The primary direction is extending the platform from
\nto\ to the detection of trace volatile organic compounds (VOCs), such as
plant-emitted VOCs, with the goal of a research publication. Discuss the
improvements this may require (e.g.\ line selection for the target VOC, higher
sensitivity, interference handling, and calibration).
